I'm a curious and aspiring AMS engineering student at Georgia Tech, with a passion for circuit design, mathematics, and philosophy. I'm currently working with Dr. Shaolan Li on high-speed pipelined and CT delta-sigma ADCs, focusing on designing a two-stage feedforward OTA for a continuous-time integrator. Previously, I worked as an RF IC Design intern at Skyworks, optimizing the low-noise amplifier of the front-end system, and published a senior thesis at the University of Illinois on wideband amplification techniques for 6G communication systems.
